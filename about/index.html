About

Startup speed. Corporate scale.

Playground Ventures works at the intersection of startup speed and corporate scale. Our team has spent years inside both new-to-world CPG brands and large corporate venture studios – which means we know exactly where good ideas get stuck, and how to get them moving again.

We've supported early-stage brands finding their first customers, and helped innovation teams inside large food & beverage organizations build the muscle to move like founders – through consumer insights, commercialization, digital experimentation, and hands-on operating support.

The name isn't an accident. We think the best innovation work happens when people are given real room to play – to test, to fail cheaply, and to find what actually works before betting big on it.

Who you're working with

Playground is two operators, not a firm with a bench. One has sat in the buyer's chair. The other has run the research that tells you what people will actually buy. We built the same brand together, from zero.

Ian Abbott

Co-Founder

Ian has spent his career on both sides of the retail desk, which is the rare part.

He spent a decade inside retail: Director of Merchandising at Supervalu, a buyer at Target across carbonated beverages and private-label meat, seafood and produce, and Director of Meat, Seafood, Deli and Bakery at Topco, where he ran roughly $2 billion in category programs and built brand architecture across premium, national-brand-equivalent and value tiers.

Then he crossed over and started building the brands instead. He founded Revolution Brands, a platform for launching new grocery brands that guaranteed initial distribution and de-risked the traditional investment model, raising more than $4 million, launching three brands, acquiring a fourth, and winning over 2,000 points of distribution. He went on to co-found Carbe Diem inside General Mills' venture studio, taking a new-to-world pasta brand from zero to a $16 million run rate in 22 months, into more than 3,000 doors, with 34% aided awareness in under two years.

Santi Strasser

Co-Founder

Santi builds the evidence that tells you whether an idea is real before you spend a year finding out.

He spent six years at General Mills across global consumer insights for salty snacks innovation, brand experience measurement, and applied analytics for the Meals and Baking portfolio, then co-founded Carbe Diem inside the company's venture studio and helped carry it from a slide to a multi-million-dollar brand on shelf.

Before that he ran on-premise trade marketing for Grupo Petrópolis, Brazil's second-largest brewer, and grew a small HVAC business in Minneapolis by 40%. Academically, he TA'd strategic marketing, pricing strategy, brand management, and behavioral economics at the University of Minnesota's Carlson School, where he earned an MS in Business Analytics.
